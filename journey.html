Our Story So Far

Our journey

Every fixed point below is a real date — the path we've followed since 7th June.

  1. 7 June

    The first step

    Project Polaris began with a WhatsApp community of our founder and 2–3 friends.

  2. 12 June

    Aaj Ka Gyan

    We launched our daily-facts initiative inside the community, alongside our first content on Instagram and LinkedIn.

  3. 2 July

    First session

    "Fundamentals of Rocket Development" with Mr. Prakhar Vishwakarma (Missile Man of MP) — 5 participants.

  4. 12 July

    Second session

    A session with Mr. Ankit Gupta (ISRO scientist) — 22 participants.

  5. 26 July

    Cosmic Conversations

    Storytelling, a quiz and a constellation-hunting challenge — 10 to 12 participants.

  6. 3 Aug

    28+ members strong

    3 core members, 11 associates and 8 volunteers — running weekly sessions, Aaj Ka Gyan, Saturday Polls, and our volunteer program.
