Get Involved

Build the future with us.

Project Polaris is powered by students, educators, mentors and innovators who believe learning should go beyond the classroom. Whether you want to learn, lead, teach or collaborate, there's a place for you in our community.

✦

Why join Project Polaris?

  • Learn Beyond Textbooks

    Gain practical knowledge through expert sessions, workshops, research and real-world projects.

  • Develop Future-Ready Skills

    Strengthen leadership, communication, teamwork, research, critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

  • Work on Meaningful Projects

    Collaborate with passionate students to build innovative solutions and create real impact.

  • Connect With Inspiring People

    Meet students, professionals, mentors and educators who share your curiosity and ambition.

  • Build Your Portfolio

    Showcase your contributions through projects, certificates, leadership roles and community initiatives.

✦

Opportunities

Become a Volunteer

Help organise events, create content, manage operations, conduct research, or support outreach activities. Perfect for students who want to contribute while learning.

Join Our Team

Take ownership of departments, lead initiatives, manage projects and shape the future of Project Polaris. Perfect for dedicated students who enjoy leadership and teamwork.

Join Advisory Board

Join our Advisory Board and provide strategic guidance to empower young innovators and learners.

Become a Speaker

Inspire young minds by delivering workshops, career talks and interactive sessions in your area of expertise.

Partner With Us

Schools, organisations, startups, NGOs, companies and communities can collaborate with us to create meaningful educational experiences.

Help Us

Help us by filling out a quick feedback form — every response shapes what we build next.
