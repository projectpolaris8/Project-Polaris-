✦What is Polaris?

We believe education should create builders, not just learners.

Project Polaris is a student-led initiative dedicated to making practical, hands-on education accessible to every learner. We believe true learning goes beyond textbooks and examinations — it happens through exploration, collaboration, real-world experience, and the courage to solve meaningful problems.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional education and the skills needed to thrive in the modern world. Through expert-led workshops, career sessions, research opportunities, innovation challenges and hands-on projects, we empower students to discover their potential and turn ideas into impact.

At this stage, everything we build is centred around space science — because that's what fascinates most of the people we work with.
